# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural mapping and monitoring, such as crop and field-boundary mapping from Earth observation data.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): A [[Python]] toolkit and CLI that delineates agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Landsat, Sentinel-2, NAIP and others) or from satellite [[Embeddings]]. It uses [[Deep_Learning]] engines (YOLO11, DINOv3, Prithvi, SAM 2) and outputs field polygons as GeoParquet or GeoJSON. *Keywords*: field boundaries, segmentation, Earth Engine, foundation models, GeoAI
